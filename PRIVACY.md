# Privacy Policy — STL Atölyesi

**Last updated: 13 October 2026**

STL Atölyesi is a personal desktop application used to help its owner manage
their own Etsy shop and Pinterest sharing workflow. It is not a public service
and is not sold to other users.

## Information the application uses

When the owner chooses to connect Etsy or Pinterest, the application can use
the account and shop information, listing metadata, images, selected Pinterest
board details, and OAuth access tokens required for the features the owner has
enabled. It does not ask for or store Etsy, Pinterest, Google, or other account
passwords.

## How information is used

Information is used only to display the owner's shop data locally and, after
the owner explicitly presses the sharing button, to create Pinterest Pins for
the owner's Etsy listings. Pinterest Pin links point to the applicable Etsy
listing; Google Drive delivery links, customer information, and OAuth tokens
are not included in Pin content.

## Storage and sharing

Application records and connection credentials are stored locally on the
owner's computer. The application does not sell, rent, or disclose this
information to third parties. Information is sent to Etsy or Pinterest only
when the owner uses the corresponding connection or sharing feature.

## Control and deletion

The owner can disconnect an account and delete local application records from
their computer. Disconnecting locally does not revoke an OAuth grant at the
provider; the owner can also revoke application access from the relevant Etsy
or Pinterest account settings.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the project's public
[support page](https://github.com/pozitifadam94-tech/etsy/issues).
